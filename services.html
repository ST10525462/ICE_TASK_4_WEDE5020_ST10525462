Our Services & Programs


Youth Peer Support Groups

Weekly facilitated discussions for young adults seeking a safe space to share experiences and build coping strategies. Facilitated by trained peer leaders, these sessions offer young adults a secure space to express their feelings alongside peers who understand their journey.

Addiction Recovery & Counselling

Navigating substance abuse can feel overwhelming. We offer confidential guidance, educational materials on addiction awareness, and structured referral pathways to connect individuals directly with accredited treatment centers and counselors.

Educational Workshops & Outreach

Prevention and early intervention begin with awareness. We organize interactive mental health awareness workshops covering stress management, depression, and anxiety identification for schools and university campuses.

Family Support & Crisis Resources

Mental health challenges affect the entire family network. We provide specialized guidance for family members and guardians, equipping them with tools to navigate a loved one's crisis and manage grief.
