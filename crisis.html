Emergency Crisis Helplines

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or experiencing a severe mental health crisis, please reach out to these 24/7 free toll-free lines immediately:


  • SADAG Mental Health Helpline: 0800 567 567
  • Suicide Crisis Line: 0800 567 567
  • 24hr Substance Abuse Helpline: 0800 121 314
  • Cipla Mental Health Helpline: 0800 456 789
